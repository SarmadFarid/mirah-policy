MIR’AH Account and Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

This page explains how to request deletion of a MIR’AH account. The App is operated by Hajra Official.

How to submit a request

  1. Email contact@hajraedu.com with the subject line MIR’AH Account Deletion.
  2. Send the request from the email address registered to your MIR’AH account. In your message, state that you want your MIR’AH account and associated personal information deleted.
  3. An authorised administrator may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and account ownership, then review and process the request.

Request account deletion by email

What data is deleted?

When a verified request is processed, the account and associated personal information will be deleted, except for records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other lawful reasons. Any retained information will be limited to what is needed for that reason.

Deleting an account may remove access to courses, PDFs, announcements, and other account-based learning features. Please save any information you are entitled to keep before submitting a request.

Questions

For questions about deletion, contact contact@hajraedu.com.

See also the MIR’AH Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy — MIR’AH

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MIR’AH (the “App”) handles information when you use it to access educational courses and PDFs, view announcements or notifications, and take part in questions, replies, or other learning activities. The App is operated by Hajra Official (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features enabled in the version you use, we may handle the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, and assigned account role, such as student, teacher, or administrator.
  • Learning and access information: course enrolments or access, course/section/lecture activity needed to provide the service, and subscription or access records such as plan name, payment status, or expiry date where these are used by the App.
  • Questions and learning content: questions, replies, and other content you submit through Q&A or learning/session features. This content may be visible to teachers or administrators and, depending on the feature and its settings, other authorised participants. If an anonymous-question feature is provided, anonymity may apply to other students and does not necessarily hide your identity from authorised teachers or administrators.
  • Notification information: a push-notification token or similar technical identifier may be processed if push notifications are enabled in the App.
  • Technical and security information: service logs and limited technical details generated when the App connects to our servers, where available and needed for security, troubleshooting, and reliable operation.

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive personal information in questions, replies, or other free-text content.

2. How We Use Information

We use information, where applicable, to:

  • create and manage accounts and authenticate users;
  • provide courses, PDFs, announcements, Q&A, and other learning features;
  • manage course access and subscription or payment-status records;
  • deliver notifications where enabled;
  • protect the App, investigate misuse, troubleshoot issues, and maintain service reliability; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared or made available as needed for the following purposes:

  • Teachers and administrators: authorised people may access account, learning-access, or participation information to manage students and provide the educational service.
  • Other authorised participants: questions, replies, and other content may be visible to people permitted to see them by the relevant feature and its settings.
  • Service providers: hosting, database, and infrastructure providers may process information on our behalf to operate the App. If Firebase Cloud Messaging or another push-notification service is used, the relevant provider may process technical information needed to deliver notifications.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, or secure the service.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the App, provide course access, maintain service security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we will delete it or take reasonable steps to remove identifying details, subject to lawful retention requirements and limited backup or security-retention periods.

6. Account and Data Deletion

MIR’AH does not currently provide self-service account deletion. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email contact@hajraedu.com using the email address associated with your account, and use the subject “MIR’AH Account Deletion.” Please include your account email address in the message. We may request reasonable information to verify that you control the account.

An authorised administrator will review and process verified requests. When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted unless particular records must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other lawful reasons. Where retention is necessary, we will limit retained information to what is needed for that reason.

You can also visit the MIR’AH Account and Data Deletion page for these instructions.

7. Children’s Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the App in a way that should not have occurred, contact us using the details below so we can review the request. The App’s intended age group and any applicable child-safety requirements should be accurately reflected in the store listing and operating practices.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to or correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and the needs of the service. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date when a revised version is published. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
